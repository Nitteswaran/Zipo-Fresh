Our Story

More Than a Grocery Store

Zipo Fresh was built on one simple belief: every family in Ipoh deserves access to truly fresh, quality groceries — right in their neighbourhood, every day of the year.

Located at No 751, Jalan Tasek, Taman Musim Bunga, we've become the go-to store for residents who value freshness, convenience, and a friendly face behind the counter.

We receive fresh produce daily so you always get peak quality.
We're open 7AM–9PM every single day — no exceptions.
We support local Perak farmers and suppliers where possible.
We believe good groceries shouldn't require a long drive or a big budget.
