Who We Are

Serving Ipoh
With Freshness

Zipo Fresh was founded on one powerful conviction: that every family in Ipoh deserves access to genuinely fresh, high-quality groceries — available in their own neighbourhood, every day of the year.

Nestled at No 751, Jalan Tasek, Taman Musim Bunga, we've grown from a neighbourhood grocery into a trusted daily staple for countless families in Ipoh and the surrounding Perak community.

We open our doors at 7:00 AM every single day — because we believe fresh groceries shouldn't wait. From morning shoppers to evening runs, we're here when you need us.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Freshness First

We receive fresh stock daily. Every morning our team ensures produce is at peak quality before it reaches our shelves. No compromises.

Community Roots

Zipo Fresh was built for this neighbourhood. We know our customers, we listen, and we stock what Ipoh families actually need.

Local & Sustainable

We prioritise local Perak suppliers where possible — reducing food miles, supporting local farmers, and keeping it genuinely fresh.

Fair Prices

Quality groceries should be accessible. We keep our prices competitive so every family in Ipoh can eat well without overspending.

Always Open

7AM to 9PM, every single day — including weekends and public holidays. We're reliable because you need us to be.

Personal Service

We're a neighbourhood store, not a faceless chain. We treat every customer like a neighbour, because you are.
