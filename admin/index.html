How to Use This Admin

📰 Step 1 — Write a Post

Go to "New Post" in the sidebar. Fill in all the fields. For the content, you can use simple HTML tags like <p>, <h2>, <strong>, <em>, <ul>, <blockquote>. Click "Save Post".

📦 Step 2 — Export

Go to "Export Data". Click "Copy to Clipboard". This gives you the complete posts.js file content with all your posts.

💾 Step 3 — Publish

Open data/posts.js in any text editor (Notepad works fine). Paste the copied content, replacing everything. Save. Done — your post is live!

💡 SEO Tips for Posts

  • Include "Ipoh" and "Zipo Fresh" in your title naturally
  • Keep your excerpt to 1–2 sentences, keyword-rich
  • Use one <h2> for each main section of your post
  • Mention the store address or location in at least one post paragraph
  • Post regularly — even monthly is better than never
  • Topics that rank well: seasonal produce, local tips, healthy eating, Malaysian food
